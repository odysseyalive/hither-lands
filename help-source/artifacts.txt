{tile:capital:O| }           Of the Great Treasures
            ======================
            "In that time were made those things that afterwards were most
            renowned of all the works of the Elves."
            -- The Silmarillion, ch. 7
            The great works of the Age lie hidden in Beleriand, waiting.

What each one does you must learn for yourself, and '{key:Examine an item}'
will tell you what you have learned; but their names are already legend.  A
few of those you may find:

  Ringil          - Fingolfin's ice-bright sword, that gave
        Morgoth seven wounds before the High King fell.
  Angrist         - "Iron-cleaver," Telchar's knife, which cut a
        Silmaril from the Iron Crown and snapped.
  Gurthang        - "Iron of Death," the black sword of Túrin,
        that speaks, and does not forget.
  Anguirel        - one of Eöl's two blades of meteor-iron, worn
        by Maeglin of Gondolin.
  Aranrúth        - "King's Ire," the sword of Thingol of Doriath.
  Glamdring and   - the paired blades of Gondolin, Foe-hammer and
  Orcrist           Goblin-cleaver, that burn cold when orcs come.
  The Dragon-helm - the Helm of Hador, crowned with Glaurung's
  of Dor-Lómin      likeness; Morgoth's servants fled it.
  Belthronding    - the black bow of Beleg Cúthalion, truest
        friend that Túrin had.
  The Elessar     - the green Elfstone of healing, wrought in
        Gondolin, whose fame outlives the Age.
  Nimphelos       - the great pearl Thingol paid to the Dwarves
        of Belegost.
  Camlost         - "Empty-handed," gauntlets that remember the
        hand of Beren, and what it held.

Weapons and armour of the Enemy's making lie below also -- black
things of meteor-iron and worse.  Whether to wield them is your
own affair.

The smiths of the Age worked in ranks of matter: dwarf-iron grey
and runed, elven steel blue-white, meteor-iron black with a glint
of stars, and rarest of all, work that saw the Light of Valinor
itself.
