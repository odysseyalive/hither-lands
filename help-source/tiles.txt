Appendix III: Of the Painted Tiles
==================================

Hither Lands draws the whole game -- terrain, monsters, objects,
and you -- as painted tiles in a single warm palette, made for
this First-Age setting.  This file covers what the tileset adds
and how to turn it on.

Turning tiles on
----------------

Tiles work in the SDL2 interface only (never in the terminal).
Start the game with the SDL2 frontend, load or create a
character, and once you stand at the main command prompt open the
menu bar at the top of the window and follow it through

    FAangband -> Tiles -> Set -> Hither Lands

Then under Tiles -> Size, keep the width about twice the height
(width 6, height 3 suits the 10x20 font).  Your choice is saved
and holds for later sessions.

What the tileset adds
---------------------

  Animated tiles     - torchlight flickers and water shimmers on
                       their own, even while the game waits for
                       your move.  No option needed.
  Shapechange        - take another form (a Beorning's bear, a
  sprites              druid's shapes, the '{key:Change shape}'
                       command) and your figure on the map
                       changes with you.
  Per-race sprites   - you appear as your own race, and so do the
                       people of the Free Races you meet below;
                       a female-flagged foe shows a female figure.
  Your own figure    - the player sprite's gender is chosen at
                       character generation, on the step between the
                       map and race menus, and kept in your savefile.

What stays ASCII
----------------

The disposition colour-tints described in Of Companions on the Road
belong to ASCII mode; with tiles on, read dispositions with the
look command and the monster list instead.  The
'{key:Identify symbol}' command always answers in symbols, whatever
is drawn on the map.
