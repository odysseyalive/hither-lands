{tile:capital:O|O}           f Beasts and Fell Things
            =========================
            "...thus did Melkor breed the hideous
            race of the Orcs in envy and mockery
            of the Elves..."
                    -- The Silmarillion, ch. 3
            Beleriand is not empty, and little of
            what fills it wishes you well.  One
who can name a thing before it reaches him lives longer than one
who cannot, so the kindreds are set out below with the mark each
bears upon the map.

Look before you strike.  Press '{key:Look around}' and the cursor will
walk from creature to creature, naming each and telling what is known
of it.  '{key:Identify symbol}' will name a symbol you do not know, and
'{key:Display visible monster list}' lists everything presently in your
sight.  Not all of it is your enemy; of neutral beasts and of those who
will walk with you, see Of Companions on the Road.

Some creatures wandered in from stranger legends than this one; take
them as you find them.

The great names
---------------

Deep in Angband wait the named terrors of the Age, among them
Gothmog lord of Balrogs, Glaurung father of dragons, Ungoliant,
Sauron of the werewolves, and Carcharoth at the gate.  At the
uttermost depth sits Morgoth Bauglir, the Iron Crown, whom all
this war is for.

Where these stand in the tale of years is told in Of the Years.
