{tile:capital:O| }           Of Beleriand and Its Realms
            ===========================
            "...there were mighty convulsions in the earth, and Beleriand
            was broken and laid waste..."
            -- The Silmarillion, ch. 24
            That breaking is not yet come upon the land, and until it comes

there is a whole world to walk.  The wilderness spreads Beleriand and the
lands east of it -- plains, forests, mountains, rivers, swamps, and deserts
-- and the great dungeons are sunk into it where the tales say they stood.

Of the roads between
--------------------

Region is joined to region by paths, and a path is worked with the
same keys as a stair.  Stand upon one and press
'{key:Go up staircase}' toward the safer country, or
'{key:Go down staircase}' toward the danger, for depth is danger here
as it is below.  Run as Appendix I directs, step warily with
'{key:Walk}', and press '{key:Start exploring}' to be drawn on toward
what you have not yet seen.  There is no magic carriage; you walk, as
Beren walked.

By day the wild is lit, and by night you must carry your own light --
though by night the beasts of the wilderness are sleeping.  Towns
keep their own hours.

Of the four worlds
------------------

Four world-maps are offered you at birth.  The standard and the
extended wildernesses hang the dungeons from an overworld of paths;
the hybrid game walks the dungeons in sequence through portals; and
the plain Angband game keeps one town and one long stair down, with
Sauron near the bottom of it, and Morgoth at the last.

Five dungeons anchor the map -- Amon Rûdh of the Petty-Dwarves,
Nargothrond upon the guarded plain, Nan Dungortheb beneath the
Mountains of Terror, Tol-in-Gaurhoth in the Vale of Sirion, and
Angband itself behind the Gasping Dust.  Each keeps its guardian, and
their tales are told in the Gazetteer.

Of coming home
--------------

A scroll of Word of Recall will snatch you out of the wild or out of
the deeps and set you down in your home town, though not at once; and
read at home it will carry you back down to the furthest point of
your daring.  Read a second scroll to change your mind.  Should you
come to another town and like it better, press '{key:Move house}' and
your house moves there.
