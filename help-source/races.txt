{tile:capital:O| }           Of the Peoples of the West
            ==========================
            "Now the Children of Ilúvatar are Elves and Men, the Firstborn
            and the Followers."
            -- The Silmarillion, ch. 1
            Thirteen peoples may take up the war against Morgoth, and no two
            of them go at it alike.  Those marked (b) suit a traveller new
            to

the road; those marked (e) ask some seasoning.  Each keeps its own
home town in the wilderness game.

The Elves
---------

  High-Elf (e)   - Noldor of Gondolin and Nargothrond, mightiest
                   and most burdened; masters of every skill, they
                   see the unseen from birth and start above first
                   level, at the steepest experience cost.
  Grey-Elf (e)   - Sindar of Thingol's Doriath; strong of arm and
                   will, at home under trees, unharmed by light.
  Green-Elf (e)  - Laiquendi of Ossiriand; hunters and singers,
                   keen-eyed, silent, deadly with bows, slight in
                   close battle.
  Dark-Elf       - the Moriquendi who never saw the Light; the
                   stealthiest of all folk, but frail, and the sun
                   pains them.

The Dwarves and the Halflings
-----------------------------

  Dwarf          - the Broadbeams of Belegost; strong, tough,
                   never blinded, resistant to spellcraft -- and
                   loud as a forge underfoot.
  Longbeard      - Durin's Folk of Khazad-dûm, greatest of the
                   Seven Houses; all a Dwarf's strengths written
                   larger.
  Petty-Dwarf    - the Noegyth Nibin, Mîm's hunted kindred; the
                   finest handlers of magical devices alive, free
                   of movement, and easily broken.
  Hobbit (b)     - halflings out of a later age; peerless with
                   slings, locks, and shadows, hardy against
                   sickness, feeble in a stand-up fight.

The Men and the Others
----------------------

  Easterling (b) - Men out of the East, the plain measure of
                   mortal kind; sturdy, adaptable, any calling.
  Adan (e)       - the Edain of the Three Houses, friends of the
                   Eldar; surpassing other Men in every field, and
                   their endurance cannot be worn down.
  Drúadan (e)    - the Drúedain, allies of the Haladin; wise,
                   quiet, tough, seeing minds from afar, though
                   towns mistrust them.
  Beorning (b)   - skin-changers of the Anduin vales; hardy
                   woodsfolk who may take bear-shape with the
                   '{key:Change shape}' command.
  Ent            - the Onodrim, shepherds of trees; terribly
                   strong, slow-handed, and wary of fire above all
                   things.

Choose as you will.  The stat tables, the experience costs, and
the full particulars are set out on the race screen at birth as
you scroll.
