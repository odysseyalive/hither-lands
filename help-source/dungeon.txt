{tile:capital:O| }           Of Delvings, and How to Walk in Them
            ====================================
            Beneath every dungeon-marked hill and fortress of the map lie
            levels without end.  Stand upon a down-stair and press
            '{key:Go down staircase}' to descend; '{key:Go up staircase}'
            climbs back toward the light.  A level once left is gone

forever, for the deeps are vaster than any map, so take what you need the
first time. Most levels offer one stair up and two down.

Light
-----

Without light you are blind, and there is no reading, no casting, no
picking of locks, no seeing of traps.  Carry a torch or a lamp and
spare fuel with it, and refuel with '{key:Fuel your light source}'
before the dark surprises you.  Rooms may be lit or unlit, and the
deeper you go the darker they are.  Necromancers alone walk
lightless, by choice and by art.

Traps and doors
---------------

Traps lie hidden until your wits uncover them -- a passive skill,
for there is no search command -- or until you blunder into one;
'^' marks those you know of.  Disarm with
'{key:Disarm a trap or chest}', and carefully.  Trap-detection magic
marks out a green zone of safety, but it is a snapshot only, and what
the monsters dig afterward it does not know.  Doors are '+' when shut
and ''' when open, and a secret door wears the look of plain granite
until you pass close by it.  Open with '{key:Open a door or a chest}'
and close with '{key:Close a door}'.

Mining
------

Quartz veins '*' hold the richest strikes, and magma '%' the lesser.
Dig as Appendix I directs, and the game will wield your best digger
unbidden.  Always carry one, for rock can close behind you, and a
shovel has saved more lives than many a sword.

Reading a level
---------------

On arrival the level tells you something of itself, a danger feeling
from "quiet and peaceful" to "omens of death", and, as you explore, a
promise of treasure from cobwebs to superb.  Rest with
'{key:Rest for a while}', survey with '{key:Full dungeon map}', and
scroll the map with '{key:Locate player on map}'.

At the last depth of Angband, past every terror of the descent,
Morgoth himself bars the way down.  There is no level beneath him.
There does not need to be.
