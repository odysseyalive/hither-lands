{tile:capital:O| }           Of Skin-Changing: the Full Particulars
            ======================================
            "...he took thence the ghastly wolf-hame of Draugluin, and
            the bat-fell of Thuringwethil."
            -- The Silmarillion, ch. 19
            *** SPOILERS ***  This file lists every shape with its numbers.
            If you would rather discover them in play, close this file now.
            Of Skin-Changing tells all a traveller needs and no more.

Notation: hit/dam/AC are combat modifiers; named flags are gained
while shaped; equipment worn keeps all its own powers.

How each shape is gained
------------------------

  Druid      - Fox Form L1 [Lesser Charms]; Become Pukel-man L30,
               Eagle's Flight L35, Bear Form L40 [Creature
               Dominion].
  Necromancer- Bat Form L3 [Into the Shadows]; Warg Form L28
               [Fear and Torment]; Vampire Form L37 [Corruption
               of Spirit].
  Blackguard - Werewolf Form L32 [Fear and Torment].
  Beorning   - bear at will with '{key:Change shape}', cub below
               L20, bear to L39, great bear from L40.
  Anyone     - dragon-scale mail's activation turns you into its
               dragon; certain rarer items grant the beast-forms.

The beast-forms
---------------

  Fox          -3/-3/+3.  STR-3, Stealth+5, +1 blow, +1 move;
               feather fall, free action.  Bite and claw.
  Bear cub     +5/+5/+0.  STR+1, INT-3, infra+3; clumsy paws
               (disarming suffers).  Four light blows.
  Bear         +10/+10/+2.  STR+2, INT-2, CON+1; fearless.
               Five blows.
  Great bear   +15/+15/+5.  STR+3, INT-2, CON+2, +1 blow;
               fearless.  Seven blows -- a mill of claws.
  Eagle        +5/+0/+10.  +3 moves; flying, trap-immune, sees
               the unseen, cannot be blinded, confused, or
               frightened; free action.  Five rending blows.
  Bat          +0/-10/+10.  Speed+3, Stealth+3, infra+5; flying,
               trap-immune, sees invisible, cannot be blinded.
               Entering costs a little blood.  Two weak blows.
  Warg         +5/+5/+0.  +2 blows; entering hurts briefly,
               cures fear, and rouses a berserk fury.  Four
               savage blows.
  Vampire      +3/+3/-5.  Speed+5, Stealth+5, infra+5; sees
               invisible, holds life; your own light dims.
               Entering costs a quarter of your health; your
               bite drinks blood back.
  Werewolf     +5/+5/+0.  +1 blow, +1 move; regenerating.
               Entering terrifies the nearby, cures fear, and
               rouses fury.  Four rending blows.
  Pukel-man    +0/+5/+20.  STR+4, CON+4, save+20, damage cut by
               10, poison-proof, shard-hard, Speed-5; sustained,
               regenerating, stun-proof, holds life; cures
               poison and stunning on entry.  Four stone fists.

The dragons
-----------

All twelve dragon-shapes share one body: +0/+5/+10, Stealth-3,
devices+10, throwing-30, four heavy blows -- bite, bite, claw,
tear.  Re-activate the armour WHILE in dragon-shape to breathe.
Each colour keeps its own element, total in its kind:

  black acid  -  blue lightning  -  white frost  -  red fire  -
  green poison

and the high dragons besides: shining (light; sees invisible),
law and chaos (hold life), gold (sound; free action, unconfused),
multi-hued, balance, and power -- whose breaths are as their
names.

Practical notes
---------------

A shape replaces your skills and blows wholesale.  A warrior's
sword-arm is wasted in a fox, and a druid's fox wastes nothing.
The entry costs of bat, warg, and vampire are paid out of your own
body, so do not put on the vampire at a sliver of health.  The
Pukel-man trades speed for near-invulnerability, a shape for
standing in doorways and not for chasing.  Reverting is free;
shifting in is a turn (Beorning) or a cast.  Every shape has its
own sprite under the Hither Lands tileset, five of them painted
for this set alone.
