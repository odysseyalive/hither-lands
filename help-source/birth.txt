{tile:capital:O| }           Of Coming into Beleriand
            ========================
            "By the starlit mere of Cuiviénen, Water of Awakening, they
            rose from the sleep of Ilúvatar..."
            -- The Silmarillion, ch. 3
            Every hero of the Elder Days began somewhere, and you begin
            here.
            Six screens make a traveller of you, and the arrow keys and
ENTER
drive them all, in either keyset.

The birth screens
-----------------

  1. RACE    - choose among the thirteen peoples, of whom Of the
               Peoples of the West tells.  Stat changes and the
               experience penalty are shown as you scroll.
  2. CLASS   - choose a calling; of these see Of Callings and
               Crafts.
  3. OPTIONS - birth options are locked for your character's whole
               life.  Two notable defaults: a starting kit of gear
               (in place of extra gold), and no-selling (shops pay
               nothing, but you find more gold).
  4. STATS   - the point-based buyer is the default and comes
               pre-filled with sound choices; unspent points become
               starting gold.  The standard roller rolls instead,
               and will roll again at your word until you accept.
  5. HISTORY - edit your background story, if you wish.
  6. NAME    - last of all, the name.  End it with a Roman numeral
               ("Fred I") and your next of that line will succeed
               it automatically.

The five stats
--------------

  STR - to-hit and damage, carried weight, and extra blows with
        heavier weapons.  The fighter's early friend.
  INT - spellcasting for mages, rogues, necromancers, and
        blackguards; also magical devices and disarming.
  WIS - spellcasting for priests, paladins, druids, and rangers;
        also your saving throws against magic.
  DEX - extra blows with light weapons, to-hit, dodging, locks,
        and holding onto your purse.
  CON - hit points and recovery.  Matters most in the deep places.

Hit points come of your race and class together; spell points, for
those who cast, grow with level and with the casting stat.
Warriors never have spell points, and want none.

Where you begin
---------------

In the wilderness game each race begins in its own home town --
Grey-Elves in Menegroth, Dwarves in Belegost, and so on -- and the
folk of the more perilous lands begin above first level.  In the
dungeon-only game all begin equal, at the gate of one deep hold.

With the Hither Lands tileset your sprite is of your own race; its
gender is settled when the tileset is installed, not at birth.
