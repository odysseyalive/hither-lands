            Not all that lives in Beleriand wishes you ill.  Beasts and the
            Free
            Peoples may stand neutral, and a few will walk at your side, as

Finrod walked with the house of Barahir.  How such folk are known,
how they are won, and how they are led is set down here.


Dispositions
------------

Look at a creature with '{key:Look around}' and its temper is named
after it.

  aggressive   it means you harm.  "(unaware)" means it has not yet
               noticed you.
  neutral      it lets you be unless provoked.  Territorial animals
               are marked "territorial"; give them room.
  aligned      it is fighting your enemies, or follows you.
  following    it is one of your own followers.

The monster list, '{key:Display visible monster list}', counts your
helpers with an "(allied)" tag.  In ASCII mode neutral folk of the
Free Peoples are tinted green, and bright green when they fight for
you; territorial beasts are yellow, and other wild animals umber.
With graphics tiles these tints do not apply, so let the look command
and the monster list answer for you instead.


Parley and recruiting
---------------------

The deed is called "Recruit a monster".  In the standard keyset it
answers to 'j' -- press it, then a direction toward an ADJACENT
neutral creature.  In the roguelike keyset 'j' walks south instead,
so reach the command through the ENTER command menu, under "Hidden",
or bind a keymap of your own.

  - Who may join: neutral people of the Free Peoples, that is, the
    speaking races.  A character with the Beguile ability -- a Druid
    specialty -- may also charm wild animals that are not evil.
  - Persuasion is a contest of your level against the creature's.
    Great folk will not lightly follow the untried.
  - Fail against a much greater creature, more than five levels
    above you, and it takes offence and turns hostile.  A milder
    failure leaves it merely unconvinced, and you may try again.
  - You can lead 1 follower, plus one more at level 15 and another
    at level 30.
  - Parley takes a game turn, whether or not it succeeds.

To dismiss a follower, parley with it a second time; it leaves off
following you and returns to neutrality.


Leading allies
--------------

Allies need no orders.  A follower seeks out your enemies within its
hearing and fights them, and when no enemy is near it walks with you.
An ally never turns on you of its own will -- but strike or rob one
and it will remember, as the sons of Ulfang taught the Union of
Maedhros that a false friend is worse than an open foe.


The wilds of Beleriand
----------------------

Animals and growing things begin neutral rather than hostile.
Territorial beasts attack if you press into their ground, skittish
ones flee, and molds and mushrooms strike only what stands beside
them.  Evil things, the undead, and the creatures of Morgoth are
enemies always.  A character with the Charm ability moves among
beasts without alarming them.
