{tile:capital:O| }           Of the Years
            ============
            "...it was named Dagor Aglareb, the Glorious Battle.  A victory
            it was, and yet a warning..."
            -- The Silmarillion, ch. 13
            The game is set in the First Age of the world, in Beleriand, the
            land that lay west of the Blue Mountains.  As you go down, you
            go

back through the Age's own history: green realms and guarded
kingdoms above, ash and iron below, and at the bottom the
beginning of all the trouble.

The Age in brief
----------------

  1. The Elves awake at Cuiviénen and journey west; the
     Green-Elves turn aside and stay in Ossiriand.
  2. Fëanor makes the Silmarils.  Morgoth and Ungoliant destroy
     the Two Trees and steal them.
  3. The Noldor depart under the Doom of Mandos to make war for
     the jewels' recovery.
  4. The Siege of Angband, the long peace in which Doriath,
     Nargothrond, and hidden Gondolin flower.
  5. Dagor Bragollach, the Battle of Sudden Flame: rivers of fire
     break the Siege; Fingolfin rides to the gate and dies.
  6. The deeds of Beren and of Túrin: the Quest of the Silmaril,
     the fall of Nargothrond, the black sword's doom.
  7. Nírnaeth Arnoediad, the Unnumbered Tears, when treason
     broke the great alliance of the free peoples.
  8. Gondolin falls, betrayed from within.
  9. The War of Wrath, in which the Enemy is thrown down at
     last -- and that is where you come in.

The road down
-------------

Five appointed foes mark the descent, each a chapter of the tale:
Mîm at Amon Rûdh, Glaurung at Nargothrond, Ungoliant in Nan
Dungortheb, Sauron at Tol-in-Gaurhoth, and Morgoth himself at the
hundredth depth of Angband.  Defeat the last and the Age is
mended.  What each of their strongholds was in its own day is told
in the chapter of places.
