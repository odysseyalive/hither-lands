{tile:capital:A| }           A Gazetteer of the Deep Places
            ==============================
            "...encompassed within the Girdle of Melian."
            -- The Silmarillion, ch. 14
            The Hither Lands are Middle-earth itself, the mortal shore east
            of the Great Sea, and Beleriand is their war-torn west, where

this game is set.  What follows is what the wise remember of the places you
will walk.

The great cities
----------------

  Menegroth    - the Thousand Caves, Thingol's hall behind the
     Girdle of Melian; the fairest dwelling east of
     the Sea.
  Gondolin     - the Hidden Rock, Turgon's white city, found
     only by those it summons.
  Belegost     - the Great Fortress of the Broadbeam Dwarves,
     whose lord Azaghâl wounded Glaurung.
  Khazad-dûm   - the vast eastern mansion of Durin's Folk, in
     its morning-glory.
  Nargothrond  - Finrod's cavern-fortress above the Narog --
     in these days already a name of dread.

The strongholds of the Enemy and the deep places
------------------------------------------------

  Angband          - the Iron Prison beneath Thangorodrim, where
         Morgoth sits crowned with stolen light.
  Nan Dungortheb   - the Valley of Dreadful Death, where
         Ungoliant's brood still spins.
  Tol-in-Gaurhoth  - the Isle of Werewolves, that was fair Tol
         Sirion before Sauron took it.
  Amon Rûdh        - the Bald Hill, red-capped with seregon,
         where Mîm keeps the last halls of his kind.

The wild regions
----------------

  Anfauglith    - the Gasping Dust, that was a green plain until
      the Sudden Flame.
  Dorthonion    - the highland of pines where Barahir's outlaws
      held out, and fell.
  Doriath       - the Fenced Land, girdled by enchantment.
  Sirion Vale   - the pass and course of the great river, the
      highway of the Age.
  Talath Dirnen - the Guarded Plain, watched from Nargothrond.
  Ossiriand     - the Land of Seven Rivers, where the Green-Elves
      sing unseen.
  Fen of Serech - the reed-marsh where rearguards buy time with
      blood.

East over the Blue Mountains lie quieter lands -- Eriador, the vales
of Anduin, the Gladden Fields -- out of which the humbler peoples
come.  And some names upon the map belong to later songs than this
one; walk them gently, for they are visiting, as you are.
