{tile:capital:O|O}           f Havens and Markets
            ====================
            You will come up out of the wild at dusk, most times, with the
            weight all wrong on your back and blood on something, and you
            will see lamps.  That is a town.  There is no wall worth the
            name and no watch that will stop you, and the first thing you

hear is somebody arguing about a price.

The towns of the free peoples shelter four or nine numbered doors, and the
townsfolk between them -- urchins, drunks, and worse, hardly worth a blow,
and sometimes worth some care for your purse.

Nobody in a town will ask where you have been.  They can tell.  What they
want to know is what you brought back, and whether you can pay, and the
second question is the one they ask out loud.

The shops
---------

The doors are numbered rather than named, which offends the poets and suits
everyone else.  Walk onto a numbered door to go in.

  {tile:shop:general|1}     General Store
        food, torches, oil, cloaks, picks and shovels; the
        town's dependable larder.

  {tile:shop:armoury|2}     Armoury
        mail and shields, richer as you prove
        yourself below.

  {tile:shop:weaponsmith|3}     Weaponsmith
        blades, bows, and ammunition, and the loudest
        forge on the street to find it by.

  {tile:shop:bookseller|4}     Bookseller
        the simpler books of every realm, shelved by a
        man who has read none of them.

  {tile:shop:alchemist|5}     Alchemist
        potions and scrolls, Word of Recall among them,
        which is the errand you will keep coming back for.

  {tile:shop:magic|6}     Magic Shop
        rings, amulets, wands, and staves; sell a spent
        wand and buy it back recharged.

  {tile:shop:black market|7}     Black Market
        anything at all, for far too much, and no
        questions asked in either direction.

  {tile:shop:home|8}     Home
        your own storeroom, and the one honest
        price in town.

The General Store and the Alchemist are the two you will wear out.  The
Black Market you will visit once, out of curiosity, and come away
insulted, and go back.

Within a shop, 'p' or 'g' buys, 's' or 'd' sells (or stashes, at
home), 'l' or 'x' examines a ware, and ESC takes you out again.

There is no haggling.  These are war years and everyone has one price.

The terms of trade
------------------

Learn the habits of shopkeepers early, because they are stranger than they
look and they are not in your favour.

By the default birth options the shops will pay nothing for your
loot, and your gold must come out of the deeps instead.  Shopkeepers
refuse what they know to be dross, and they will buy an unknown thing
with a merchant's confidence, so that selling a mystery is a fair way
to learn what it was.  Stock shifts only while you are long away;
what you sold may be gone when you return, and a shop bought empty
restocks entirely.

So a town rewards leaving it.  Go down, stay down, come back changed, and
the shelves will have changed with you.  Hover about the market for a week
and you will find the same three cloaks looking at you.

Nothing here is quite as it was in the songs -- no market of
Menegroth, no forges of Nogrod -- but a shovel bought is a shovel, in
any Age.
