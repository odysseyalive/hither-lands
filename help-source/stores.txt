{tile:capital:O| }           Of Havens and Markets
            =====================
            The towns of the free peoples shelter four or nine numbered
            doors, and the townsfolk between them -- urchins, drunks, and
            worse, hardly worth a blow, and sometimes worth some care for
            your purse.

The shops
---------

  1  General Store - food, torches, oil, cloaks, picks and
         shovels; the town's dependable larder.
  2  Armoury       - mail and shields, richer as you prove
         yourself below.
  3  Weaponsmith   - blades, bows, and ammunition.
  4  Bookseller    - the simpler books of every realm.
  5  Alchemist     - potions and scrolls, Word of Recall among
         them.
  6  Magic Shop    - rings, amulets, wands, and staves; sell a
         spent wand and buy it back recharged.
  7  Black Market  - anything at all, for far too much.
  8  Home          - your own storeroom, and the one honest
         price in town.

Within a shop, 'p' or 'g' buys, 's' or 'd' sells (or stashes, at
home), 'l' or 'x' examines a ware, and ESC takes you out again.

The terms of trade
------------------

By the default birth options the shops will pay nothing for your
loot, and your gold must come out of the deeps instead.  Shopkeepers
refuse what they know to be dross, and they will buy an unknown thing
with a merchant's confidence, so that selling a mystery is a fair way
to learn what it was.  Stock shifts only while you are long away;
what you sold may be gone when you return, and a shop bought empty
restocks entirely.

Nothing here is quite as it was in the songs -- no market of
Menegroth, no forges of Nogrod -- but a shovel bought is a shovel, in
any Age.
