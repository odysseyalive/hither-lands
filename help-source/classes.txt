{tile:capital:O| }           Of Callings and Crafts
            ======================
            "Aurë entuluva!  Day shall come again!"
            -- The Silmarillion, ch. 20
            Nine callings are open to you.  Casters draw upon INT or WIS as
            noted; Warriors draw upon nothing but themselves, as Húrin did.

The nine classes
----------------

  Warrior     - pure battle; the most blows, the best survival by
    steel alone, no spells ever.  Begins with a bonus
    specialty.
  Mage (INT)  - feeble at first, near-boundless at last; the best
    user of wands and staves in the late game.
  Priest (WIS)- servant of the Valar; prayers are GRANTED, not
    chosen, and hafted or blessed weapons please them.
  Druid (WIS) - keeper of growing things; fights unarmed, charms
    and commands beasts (of which see Of Companions on
    the Road).
  Necromancer (INT) - walks in self-made darkness that light
    disrupts; terrible power at a terrible price.
  Paladin (WIS) - the second-best fighter, praying slowly but
    striking hard; STR and WIS together.
  Rogue (INT) - the best stealth and perception; steals from
    monsters with '{key:Steal from a monster}', sets
    traps, opens what is shut.
  Ranger (WIS)- the master of the bow, with woodcraft and a fair
    sword-arm besides.
  Blackguard (INT) - a brutal brawler in heavy gear, spending
    blood for dark spellcraft; very hard to kill.

Specialties
-----------

FAangband's specialty abilities set your character apart from all
others of the same calling.  You choose one at first level
(Warriors choose two), and earn further choices as you prove
yourself against the great enemies of the Age -- three or four in
a full life.  Review yours with '{key:View abilities}'; casters
gain new spells with '{key:Gain new spells}'.

Specialties run from armsmanship, evasion, and shield mastery to
channeling, meditation, rapid fire, and stranger arts.  Two touch
the living world itself: Charm soothes the beasts of the wild so
they never turn on you unprovoked, and Beguile (a Druid specialty)
lets you charm animals to your side.  Of these also Of Companions
on the Road tells.
