            =====================
            "Aurë entuluva!  Day shall come again!"
            -- The Silmarillion, ch. 20
            A people is what you are born; a calling is what you have
            done with it since.  Every hall in Beleriand is full of
folk who chose, some of them badly.  The armoury at Barad Eithel is
kept by a man who meant to be a loremaster.  There is a Green-Elf in
Nargothrond who set down her bow forty years ago to learn the making
of staves and has not touched a bowstring since, and she is a poor
shot now and cares nothing about it.

Nine callings are open to you.  Casters draw upon INT or WIS as
noted; Warriors draw upon nothing but themselves, as Húrin did, who
stood at the Nirnaeth with an axe and no spell at all and killed until
the axe would not lift.  Whichever road you take, take it early.  The
war will not wait while you decide what you are.

The nine callings
-----------------

Three of them fight, three of them pray or study, and three walk
somewhere in between.  What follows is what the muster-rolls would
say if muster-rolls were honest.

  Warrior     - pure battle; the most blows, the best survival by
    steel alone, no spells ever.  Begins with a bonus
    specialty.  A Warrior's answer to an enchantment is
    to reach the enchanter.

  Mage (INT)  - feeble at first, near-boundless at last; the best
    user of wands and staves in the late game.  The
    early years are an apprenticeship paid in bruises,
    and many do not survive their own beginning.

  Priest (WIS)- servant of the Valar; prayers are GRANTED, not
    chosen, and hafted or blessed weapons please them.
    You will be given what is needful, which is seldom
    what you asked for.

  Druid (WIS) - keeper of growing things; fights unarmed, charms
    and commands beasts (of which see Of Companions on
    the Road).  In a country being burned from the
    north this is not the gentle calling it sounds.

  Necromancer (INT) - walks in self-made darkness that light
    disrupts; terrible power at a terrible price.  The
    dark must be carried with you and kept unbroken,
    so a well-lit room is an enemy's best defence.

  Paladin (WIS) - the second-best fighter, praying slowly but
    striking hard; STR and WIS together.  A blunt
    instrument in the hand of something that has an
    opinion about how it is used.

  Rogue (INT) - the best stealth and perception; steals from
    monsters with '{key:Steal from a monster}', sets
    traps, opens what is shut.  A Rogue leaves a hoard
    lighter and a sleeping dragon none the wiser, most
    times.

  Ranger (WIS)- the master of the bow, with woodcraft and a fair
    sword-arm besides.  The marches of Doriath and the
    pine-woods of Dorthonion were held for an Age by
    folk who shot first and were never seen at all.

  Blackguard (INT) - a brutal brawler in heavy gear, spending
    blood for dark spellcraft; very hard to kill.  The
    strength comes out of the wound, so a Blackguard
    who is not bleeding is not yet at work.

None of these is a finished thing.  Two Warriors of the same house,
armed alike, will not fight alike a year from now, and the reason is
below.

Specialties
-----------

FAangband's specialty abilities set your character apart from all
others of the same calling.  You choose one at first level (Warriors
choose two), and earn further choices as you prove yourself against
the great enemies of the Age, three or four of them in a full life.
Review yours with '{key:View abilities}'; casters gain new spells with
'{key:Gain new spells}'.

Consider them the mark the road leaves on you.  They are not bought
with gold or study but with the deaths of great foes, and there are
only so many great foes.

Specialties run from armsmanship, evasion, and shield mastery to
channeling, meditation, rapid fire, and stranger arts.  Two touch the
living world itself.  Charm soothes the beasts of the wild so they
never turn on you unprovoked, and Beguile (a Druid specialty) lets you
charm animals to your side.  Of these also Of Companions on the Road
tells.

Choose the first one at birth and you will be living with it for the
whole descent, so choose the one that covers the way you already mean
to fight, rather than the way you admire from a distance.
