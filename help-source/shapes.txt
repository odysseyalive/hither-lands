{tile:capital:O| }           Of Skin-Changing
            ================
            "Then Sauron shifted shape, from wolf to serpent, and from
            monster to his own accustomed form..."
            -- The Silmarillion, ch. 19
            Skin-changing is an old art in Middle-earth, and not the Enemy's

alone.  Some callings learn to wear a beast's form, and trade hands and
spellcraft for teeth and wings and hide.

Who shifts
----------

Druids take the shapes of beast and stone through their nature
verses; Necromancers wear the forms of the creatures of night;
Blackguards learn one savage shape of their own.  A Beorning needs
no spell at all, for bear-shape is that people's birthright, taken
at will with '{key:Change shape}', and it deepens as they grow.  And
any hero whatever, wearing the right dragon-scale armour, may briefly
know what it is to be a dragon.

Wearing another skin
--------------------

Shapes are entered by casting the form's verse with
'{key:Cast a spell}', by the Beorning's '{key:Change shape}', or by
the power of certain items.  In a shape you ARE the beast -- its
blows, its senses, its stealth, its hide.  Your worn equipment stays
on you, changed with you, and all its virtue still counts.

But paws cannot turn pages.  In beast-form your pack is closed to
you, and there is no reading, no casting, no wands, no swapping of
gear.  You may eat from the floor and little more.  Attempt anything
handier and the game asks whether to return to your own form first.

Returning
---------

Press '{key:Change shape}' to take your own shape again, and the
changing back costs nothing.  A shape is a tool.  Put it on for the
work it is good at -- travel, stealth, or slaughter -- and take it
off again to think.

With the Hither Lands tileset your figure on the map changes with
you, every form with its own sprite.

The full particulars of every shape, and there are twenty-two, are
set out with numbers and all in Of Skin-Changing: the Full
Particulars, for those who would rather know than wonder.
